Dog Encyclopedia

Beagle

Size: Small to medium

Personality: Curious, friendly, and eager to follow scents.

Care: Take daily walks and give your beagle time to sniff and explore.

Golden Retriever

Size: Large

Personality: Outgoing, gentle, and often happy to fetch.

Care: Make time for daily exercise and brush the coat regularly.

Poodle

Size: Varies by type

Personality: Alert, clever, and quick to learn new games.

Care: Offer daily play and training, and brush the curly coat regularly.
